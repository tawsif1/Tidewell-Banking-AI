# Lost or stolen cards

## Freezing a lost or stolen card
If your Tidewell Bank card is lost, stolen, or has been used without your permission, you can freeze it in chat after verifying your identity. Freezing a card takes effect immediately, and new purchases on the card are declined.

## What freezing a card does
A frozen card can't be used for new purchases. Your accounts stay open, and the money in them is not affected. Freezing a card does not cancel or replace it.

## Unfreezing or replacing a card
Cards can't be unfrozen or replaced through chat. To unfreeze a card or order a replacement card, call Tidewell Bank support at 1-800-555-0199, Monday to Friday, 8am to 8pm.

## Cancelled cards
A cancelled card can no longer be used, so it can't be frozen. If a cancelled card is lost or stolen, no further action is needed for that card.

## If someone used your card without permission
If someone used your card without permission, freeze the card first to stop any new charges. Then review your recent transactions and dispute any charges you didn't make. For more details, see "Disputing a transaction" and "Fraud and scams."
