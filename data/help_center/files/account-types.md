# Account types

## Checking accounts
A Tidewell Bank checking account is for everyday spending, bill payments, and deposits. Checking accounts come with a debit card and have a monthly account fee of $16.95.

## Savings accounts
A Tidewell Bank savings account is for setting money aside and earning interest. Savings accounts don't come with a debit card.

## Tax-Free Savings Accounts (TFSA)
A Tidewell Bank TFSA lets eligible customers save while earning tax-free growth, within the annual contribution limits set by the government. For questions about TFSA contribution limits, call Tidewell Bank support at 1-800-555-0199.

## Viewing your accounts in chat
After verifying your identity in chat, you can see all of your Tidewell Bank accounts and their current balances.

## Opening or closing an account
Accounts can't be opened or closed through chat. To open or close an account, call Tidewell Bank support at 1-800-555-0199, Monday to Friday, 8am to 8pm.
