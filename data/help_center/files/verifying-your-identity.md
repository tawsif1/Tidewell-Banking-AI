# Verifying your identity

## Why we verify your identity
Before sharing or changing any account information, Tidewell Bank confirms that you are the account holder. This protects your money and personal information from anyone trying to access your account.

## What we ask for in chat
To verify your identity in chat, Tidewell Bank asks for your full name, your date of birth, and the last 4 digits of your Tidewell Bank card. We never ask for your full card number, PIN, online banking password, or one-time passcodes.

## If verification doesn't work
If the details you provide don't match our records, you can try again. For your security, identity verification in chat is locked after three unsuccessful attempts. If this happens, call Tidewell Bank support at 1-800-555-0199, Monday to Friday, 8am to 8pm.

## Questions you can ask without verifying
You can ask general questions about Tidewell Bank's policies, fees, and services without verifying your identity. Verification is only required to view or change information about your own accounts.
