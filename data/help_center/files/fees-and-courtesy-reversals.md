# Fees and courtesy reversals

## Fee schedule
Tidewell Bank charges the following fees: an overdraft fee of $45.00, an NSF (non-sufficient funds) fee of $48.00, a wire transfer fee of $25.00, and a monthly account fee of $16.95 on checking accounts.

## Courtesy reversals
As a courtesy, Tidewell Bank can reverse one overdraft or NSF fee every 12 months (365 days). To be eligible, the fee must have been charged within the last 90 days. You can request a courtesy reversal in chat after verifying your identity.

## Fees that aren't eligible for a courtesy reversal
Wire transfer fees and monthly account fees aren't eligible for courtesy reversals. Overdraft and NSF fees charged more than 90 days ago also aren't eligible.

## If you've already had a courtesy reversal
Only one courtesy reversal is available every 12 months. If you've had a courtesy reversal in the last 12 months, you become eligible again 12 months after the date of that reversal.

## What happens when a fee is reversed
A reversed fee is credited back immediately to the account it was charged to. You receive a reference number starting with FR, and the refund appears in your transactions as a separate credit, such as "Overdraft Fee Reversal." The original fee also stays in your transaction history.

## If you think a fee was charged in error
If you believe a fee was charged by mistake and it isn't eligible for a courtesy reversal, call Tidewell Bank support at 1-800-555-0199, Monday to Friday, 8am to 8pm.
