# Contacting support and escalations

## Contacting Tidewell Bank support
Tidewell Bank support can be reached at 1-800-555-0199, Monday to Friday, 8am to 8pm.

## Speaking with a specialist from chat
If you'd like to speak with a person, you can ask at any time in chat. We'll create a support ticket with a summary of your conversation, so you won't need to explain everything again. You'll receive a reference number starting with ESC.

## When a specialist will contact you
For verified customers, a specialist calls the phone number on file. Suspected fraud is urgent, and a specialist calls within 1 hour. High-priority issues receive a call within 4 business hours, and all other requests within 1 business day.

## If your identity couldn't be verified
If your identity couldn't be verified in chat, we can still create a support ticket, but we can't call you back. Call Tidewell Bank support at 1-800-555-0199 and quote your reference number.

## What chat can help with
After verifying your identity in chat, you can view your accounts and recent transactions, freeze a card, dispute a charge, and request a courtesy fee reversal. For anything else, a specialist can help.
