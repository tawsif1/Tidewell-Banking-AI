# Privacy and security

## How we protect your account in chat
Tidewell Bank verifies your identity before sharing or changing any account information. We only share information about your own accounts, and never about another customer's accounts, even if you know their details.

## Information we'll never ask for
Tidewell Bank will never ask for your full card number, PIN, online banking password, or one-time passcodes, in chat or anywhere else.

## Keeping your information safe
Don't share your card details, PIN, or passwords with anyone, including people who say they work for Tidewell Bank. Use a strong, unique password for online banking.

## If you think your account was accessed without permission
If you think someone accessed your account without permission, freeze your card in chat and call Tidewell Bank support at 1-800-555-0199 right away. See "Fraud and scams" for more steps.
