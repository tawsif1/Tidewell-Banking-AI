# Overdraft and NSF fees explained

## What an overdraft fee is
An overdraft fee of $45.00 is charged when Tidewell Bank approves a payment even though your account doesn't have enough money to cover it, leaving your balance below zero.

## What an NSF fee is
An NSF (non-sufficient funds) fee of $48.00 is charged when a payment is declined or returned because your account doesn't have enough money to cover it.

## How to avoid overdraft and NSF fees
To avoid overdraft and NSF fees, check your balance before large purchases, and remember that pending transactions will reduce your balance when they post. Keeping a small buffer in your checking account helps cover unexpected charges.

## Getting an overdraft or NSF fee refunded
Eligible overdraft and NSF fees can be refunded through a courtesy reversal, available once every 12 months for fees charged within the last 90 days. See "Fees and courtesy reversals."
