# Pending and posted transactions

## What a pending transaction is
A pending transaction is a purchase or payment that has been authorized but not yet finalized. The amount is held from your available balance until the transaction posts.

## When pending transactions post
Most pending transactions post within a few business days. The final amount can differ from the pending amount, for example with hotel, gas station, or restaurant charges that include holds or tips.

## Disputing a pending transaction
Pending transactions can't be disputed until they post. If a charge still looks wrong after it posts, you can dispute it within 60 days of the transaction date.

## Viewing your transactions in chat
After verifying your identity in chat, you can see your transactions from the last 30 days, or from up to the last 90 days. Each transaction shows whether it's pending or posted.
