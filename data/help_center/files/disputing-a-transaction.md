# Disputing a transaction

## When you can dispute a charge
You can dispute a posted charge on your Tidewell Bank account if you didn't make it, didn't receive what you paid for, were charged twice, or were charged the wrong amount. Disputes must be filed within 60 days of the transaction date.

## Pending charges
Pending charges can't be disputed until they post, which usually takes a few business days. Many charges that look wrong while pending, such as hotel or gas station holds, are corrected automatically when they post.

## What happens after you file a dispute
When you file a dispute, you receive a case number starting with DSP. A specialist reviews every dispute and contacts you within 10 business days. Each charge can only be disputed once; if a dispute already exists for a charge, we'll share its case number and status.

## Unauthorized charges
If you dispute a charge because you didn't make it, Tidewell Bank also recommends freezing the card used for the charge, so no further unauthorized purchases can be made.

## Fees can't be disputed
Fees, such as overdraft or NSF fees, can't be disputed. Eligible overdraft and NSF fees can be refunded through a courtesy reversal instead. See "Fees and courtesy reversals."

## Charges older than 60 days
Charges older than 60 days can't be disputed through chat. To ask about an older charge, call Tidewell Bank support at 1-800-555-0199, Monday to Friday, 8am to 8pm.

## Deposits and incoming payments
Deposits, transfers into your account, and other incoming payments can't be disputed, because no money left your account.
