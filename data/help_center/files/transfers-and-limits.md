# Transfers and limits

## Transfers between your own accounts
You can move money between your own Tidewell Bank accounts in online banking at no cost. Transfers between your own accounts are usually immediate.

## Wire transfers
Wire transfers send money to other banks, including banks in other countries. Each wire transfer has a fee of $25.00, and wire transfer fees aren't eligible for courtesy reversals.

## Transfer limits
You can send up to $3,000 per day to other people, and up to $10,000 per day by wire transfer. To ask about a higher limit, call Tidewell Bank support at 1-800-555-0199.

## Transfers in chat
Transfers can't be made through chat. To move or send money, use online banking or call Tidewell Bank support at 1-800-555-0199, Monday to Friday, 8am to 8pm.
