# Fraud and scams

## What Tidewell Bank will never ask for
Tidewell Bank will never ask for your full card number, PIN, online banking password, or a one-time passcode, whether by phone, email, text, or chat. In chat, we only ask for your full name, date of birth, and the last 4 digits of your card to verify your identity.

## Signs of a scam
Be cautious of messages that create urgency, ask you to move money to "protect" it, ask for passcodes, or claim to be from Tidewell Bank but come from an unfamiliar number or address. When in doubt, contact Tidewell Bank directly at 1-800-555-0199.

## If you suspect fraud on your account
If you see charges you don't recognize, freeze your card right away, then dispute any unauthorized charges within 60 days of the transaction date. If you've found several unauthorized charges, or charges older than 60 days, our fraud team can review your account. Suspected fraud is treated as urgent, and a fraud specialist calls verified customers within 1 hour.

## If you shared information with a scammer
If you shared your card details, PIN, password, or a passcode with someone, freeze your card and call Tidewell Bank support at 1-800-555-0199 immediately.
